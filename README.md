# 🚨 Information System Services Management (MIS) - 5-Hour Emergency Exam Prep Portal

An ultra-fast, modern, single-page offline revision portal built for **Information System Services Management** based on *Management Information Systems: Managing the Digital Firm* (17th Global Edition by Jane P. Laudon & Kenneth C. Laudon).

Runs instantly in any browser simply by opening `index.html` (zero npm, zero build step, pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript).

---

## ⚡ Key Features

1. **🚨 5-Hour Emergency Cram Radar**:
   - 20 Guaranteed, high-probability recurring exam topics and definitions.
   - For each topic: *Why It Appears*, *Quick 30-Second Summary*, *Full-Credit Key Phrases & Marking Criteria*, and *High-Yield Takeaway*.
   - Interactive confidence checklist with `localStorage` persistence.

2. **⚡ Rapid-Fire Interactive Quiz**:
   - 50 authentic past-paper MCQs covering Chapters 1 through 15.
   - Real-time instant visual feedback (Green for correct, Red for incorrect).
   - In-depth pedagogical explanations with distractor analysis explaining why each wrong option is invalid.
   - "Review Mistakes Only" toggle filter to re-test only missed questions.
   - Live accuracy tracker and answer streak counter.

3. **✍️ Section B / Theory "Full-Credit" Templates**:
   - 10 complete worked solutions with marking schemes:
     1. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Calculation & Cloud vs. On-Premises Migration
     2. Capital Budgeting: Payback Period & Net Present Value (NPV) with Discount Factors
     3. Relational Database Normalization Case (1NF $\rightarrow$ 2NF $\rightarrow$ 3NF) & Anomalies
     4. Supply Chain Dynamics: The Bullwhip Effect, Root Causes & IT Solutions (CPFR, VMI)
     5. SDLC Stages & Conversion Strategies Evaluation Matrix (Parallel, Direct Cutover, Pilot, Phased)
     6. Cybersecurity Defense-in-Depth, CIA Triad, and Incident Response Framework (RPO & RTO)
     7. Porter's Value Chain Model & Strategic IS Alignment (Primary vs Support Activities & Value Web)
     8. Ethical, Social, and Legal Case Analysis (Laudon's 5 Steps & 6 Candidate Principles)
     9. Herbert Simon's Decision-Making Model & Business Intelligence Architecture (OLAP, DSS, ESS)
     10. Global Business Strategies & Systems Configuration Matrix
   - Mathematical formulas rendered via KaTeX.

4. **📖 Searchable Cheat Sheet & Formula Glossary**:
   - 47 core formulas, laws (Moore's Law, Metcalfe's Law, Mass Storage), acronyms, and standard definitions.
   - Real-time search bar with keyboard shortcut (`/`).
   - One-click copy buttons.

5. **⏱️ Adrenaline Countdown Timer**:
   - Live 5-hour countdown timer with audio-visual adrenaline pulse.
